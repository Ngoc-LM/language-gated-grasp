\section{Conclusion}
The language gate raises the $H$ of a FiLM-conditioned GR-ConvNet from 46.7 to 62.7. The text-free control attributes 9.4 of these 16.0 points to reading the prompt, including 69\% of the unseen gain. Training on 3.6\% of the images, two seeds and a $14{\times}14$ gate limit the evidence; full-data training, part-level gate supervision and the LGD protocol are next.
