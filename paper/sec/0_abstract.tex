\begin{abstract}
Language-driven grasp detection places a grasp on the object part named by a free-form prompt. The proposed method steers the GR-ConvNet grasp CNN with FiLM and a \emph{language gate}: a spatial map of cosine similarities between frozen CLIP patch tokens and the prompt embedding. On the annotated pairs of the official LGD test split of Grasp-Anything++, the model reaches 68.6\% seen and 57.7\% unseen success (two-seed mean), against 54.8\% and 40.7\% with FiLM alone. A control gate that receives the same CLIP features but not the prompt scores 11.6 and 11.8 unseen points lower in the two seeds ($p<10^{-7}$), so prompt-dependent gating gives 9.4 of the 16.0-point gain in harmonic mean over FiLM. Code: \url{https://github.com/Ngoc-LM/language-gated-grasp}.
\end{abstract}
