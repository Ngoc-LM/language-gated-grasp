\section{Experiments}
\textbf{Protocol.} Training uses 35,927 Grasp-Anything++ scenes (183,559 prompt--grasp pairs) and validation 734 scenes. Testing follows the official LGD seen/unseen split, restricted to annotated pairs: 1,376 seen and 601 unseen from 727 of 803 object keys. Test scenes, and training pairs whose object name is an unseen test name, are excluded from training; 40 of the 41 seen names (1,373 of 1,376 pairs) occur in training. A top-1 grasp is correct if its IoU exceeds 0.25 and its angle error is below $30^\circ$ for some ground-truth grasp of the prompted part; $H$ is the harmonic mean of seen and unseen success. All runs use AdamW (lr $10^{-3}$, weight decay $10^{-4}$, 500 warm-up steps, cosine), fp16, batch 32 and 6 epochs on one T4 (2.5--2.7\,h); checkpoints are selected on 1,000 validation pairs.

\textbf{Results.} FiLM (B) raises $H$ by 19.1 points, CLIP features behind the text-free gate (C0) add 6.6, and letting the gate read the prompt (C) adds 9.5. Within each seed, C exceeds C0 by 11.8 unseen points and by 6.3 and 4.4 seen points; paired McNemar tests on the same test pairs give $p<10^{-3}$ in all four comparisons, and scene-resampled 95\% bootstrap intervals exclude zero (unseen: $[6.6,17.5]$ and $[5.9,17.4]$ points). In seed 1, the text-free gate raises seen success over B by 6.4 points ($p<10^{-6}$) but unseen success by only 2.8 ($p=0.21$). In the 118 multi-object test scenes, C is correct for two prompts on different objects in 54 and C0 in 40 (seed 0, \cref{fig:qual}). Prompt-dependent gating therefore accounts for 69\% of the B$\to$C gain on unseen objects, 59\% in $H$ and 38\% on seen objects. Gate supervision (D) does not differ significantly from C (seed 0; $p=0.06$ seen, $0.24$ unseen), so C is retained.

\textbf{Cost and limitations.} C adds 0.33M trained parameters to B and a frozen 149M-parameter CLIP (12.5\,ms per frame with a cached prompt), and trains on 3.6\% of the images. A already exceeds the 18 unseen that LGD~\cite{vuong2024language} reports for GR-ConvNet with CLIP, so LGD's 48/42 are not compared.
