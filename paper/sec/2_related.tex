\section{Related Work}
Generative grasp CNNs~\cite{morrison2018closing,kumra2020antipodal} regress dense grasp maps. CLIPort~\cite{shridhar2022cliport} and CROG~\cite{tziafas2023language} use CLIP for manipulation and grasping, and LGD~\cite{vuong2024language} denoises grasps under text guidance on data built with foundation models~\cite{vuong2024grasp}. Pixel--text score maps from CLIP-style encoders drive dense prediction~\cite{zhou2022extract}; the language gate uses one as a spatial prior in a grasp CNN.
