\section{Related Work}
Generative grasp CNNs~\cite{morrison2018closing,kumra2020antipodal} regress dense grasp maps. CROG~\cite{tziafas2023language} uses CLIP for referring grasp synthesis, LGD~\cite{vuong2024language} denoises grasps under text guidance on data built with foundation models~\cite{vuong2024grasp}, and mask-guided attention~\cite{vo2024language} adds spatial language grounding to it. Pixel--text CLIP maps drive dense prediction~\cite{zhou2022extract}; the gate uses one as a spatial prior.
